## ADEQ Verification and Evidence *(majed-adeq)*

For every task, add the verification work needed to support its completion claim.

- [ ] Identify the requirement or acceptance criterion the task serves.
- [ ] Add the smallest positive test or deterministic check for the intended behavior.
- [ ] Add a negative, boundary, or failure-path check where the behavior can fail.
- [ ] Record the exact command, revision, environment, and result.
- [ ] State what the evidence does **not** prove.
- [ ] Keep unresolved, skipped, or not-run items visible in the denominator.
- [ ] Do not mark a task complete from documentation alone when code, packaging,
  or runtime behavior was the actual deliverable.

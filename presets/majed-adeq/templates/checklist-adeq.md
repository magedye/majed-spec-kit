## ADEQ Decision-Quality Checklist *(majed-adeq)*

- [ ] The intended outcome and non-goals are explicit.
- [ ] Source obligations are traceable to requirements or acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Assumptions and unknowns are labeled rather than silently filled.
- [ ] At least one reuse option was considered before building.
- [ ] Alternatives include cost, risk, reversibility, and impact.
- [ ] The selected option and its trade-offs are justified.
- [ ] Impacted components, dependencies, and recovery are identified.
- [ ] Confidence is stated with reasons and limits.
- [ ] Evidence is linked to the tested revision and environment.
- [ ] Completion claims are supported by actual evidence, not activity.
- [ ] Owner acceptance, release authorization, and host enforcement are not
  inferred from a passing test, a gate label, or a self-review.

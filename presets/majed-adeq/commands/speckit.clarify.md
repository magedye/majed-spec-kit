# ADEQ Clarification Guard

Apply these rules before and after the native clarification workflow below.

## Before asking

- Re-read the source request, the current specification, and the relevant
  repository evidence. Do not ask a question that an available source answers.
- Separate **facts** from **assumptions** and **unresolved ambiguity**.
- Ask only questions that can change a requirement, acceptance criterion,
  design choice, risk decision, or scope boundary.
- Prefer one to three high-impact questions over a long questionnaire.

## For each question

1. State why the answer matters and what it affects.
2. Offer concrete options when the choices are finite.
3. Give a recommendation and the trade-off behind it.
4. State the impact of leaving the item unresolved.
5. Record the answer in the specification; do not leave it only in chat.

## After asking

- Update the specification, obligation trace, assumptions, and confidence note.
- Preserve the requester's wording and constraints; do not silently normalize
  them into a different requirement.
- If an item remains unresolved, mark it `NEEDS CLARIFICATION` or `UNKNOWN`.
- Do not claim that clarification is complete when a material question remains.
